## 1. What we process
- **Business data** you enter: products, prices, inventory, sales, customers, staff accounts.
- **Customer data** you collect at the till: name, phone, email (only what you choose to capture).
- **Account data:** usernames, hashed passwords, roles.
- **Technical data:** device/session identifiers, logs, error reports (if monitoring is enabled).

We do **not** store card numbers. Card/QR/UPI payments are handled by your payment provider.

## 2. Where it lives
- **Desktop app:** all data stays **on your computer** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\SPOS`). We do not receive it.
- **Website/SaaS:** data is stored on the server you (or your provider) operate.

## 3. How it's used
To operate the POS: process sales, manage inventory/customers, produce receipts and reports.
We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Sharing
Only with services you enable (e.g. your payment provider, email/SMS receipt sender, error
monitoring). Each processes data under its own terms.

## 5. Retention
Business records are kept for as long as you operate the store or as required by tax/accounting law
in your country. You control deletion.

## 6. Security
Encrypted transport (HTTPS on the website), hashed passwords, role-based access, signed payment
webhooks, audit logging.
