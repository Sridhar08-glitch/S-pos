# S POS — User Guide

**Developed by Sridhar Mahalingam.** A quick guide for shop owners and staff.

## First-time setup
1. Launch S POS (desktop) or open your S POS website.
2. On the **Setup** screen, enter your **store name**, **currency**, and create your **owner
   account** (username + password). Click **Create my store**.
3. You're in. Add your products under **Products**, then start selling.

## Roles (who sees what)
- **Cashier** — Point of Sale, Sales, Customers, Gift Cards, Loyalty, Time Clock.
- **Manager** — the above + Catalog, Inventory, Purchasing, Reports.
- **Owner/Admin** — everything, including **Store Setup** and the **Advanced** tools.

Create staff under **Store Setup ▸ Users** and give each the right role and a POS PIN.

## Selling (Point of Sale)
1. **Open a register** (pick a register + opening cash) — required once per shift.
2. **Scan** a barcode/QR or **tap a product card**. Use **Custom** for an open-price item.
3. Adjust quantity, apply a **line discount / price override** (a manager PIN is asked if a
   cashier does this), or an order **discount %**.
4. Optionally pick a **customer** and a **price list**, and a **cash-rounding** rule.
5. Enter payment across **split tenders** (Cash/Card/QR/Bank/Wallet) until Paid = Total.
6. **Complete sale** → receipt (Print / Email). **Park** a sale to hold it for later.
7. **Close register** at end of shift to reconcile cash.

## Inventory
- **Products / Variants / Modifiers** — your catalog and add-ons.
- **Purchasing** — record supplier purchases and **receive** stock (partial allowed).
- **Transfers** — move stock between stores.
- **Stock Counts** — count on hand and **post** to correct inventory (variances applied).
- **Low stock** shows on the dashboard.

## Money & reports
- **Sales** — view, **refund** (partial/full) or **void**; print/email any receipt.
- **Reports** — dashboard, X (mid-shift) / Z (end-of-day), by item/cashier/hour, **CSV export**.
- **Expenses / Approvals / Reconciliation** for back-office.

## Customers & loyalty
Add customers, issue **gift cards** and **store credit**, run **loyalty** points.

## Multiple devices
The desktop app shows a network address; open it in a browser on any tablet/phone/register on the
same Wi-Fi to use S POS there too.

## Tips
- Change your currency, tax and store details under **Store Setup ▸ Companies / Tax Rates / Settings**.
- Use **Time Clock** for staff clock-in/out and shift reports.
- Everything is saved automatically; the backend is always authoritative for prices and stock.
